# #511 — the window records a transfer
- Added: a "Transfer" action on a holding row, after Write-off; it opens "Transfer to another account": date, destination account, quantity sent and received (received defaults to sent), a note.
- Added: between currencies, the rate field, prefilled with the stored rate of the date and editable; the summary gives the cost in both currencies.
- Added: the summary says what moves at cost, that no cash moves and nothing is realized, and that the shortfall is written off as a network fee at its cost.
- Journals: "Transfer out" / "Transfer in", each linking to the other account; the fee is its own write-off row, part of the transfer. Edit or delete on any of them acts on the whole transfer.
- Open point: wording in French is drafted with the code (« Transfert vers un autre compte », « Transfert sortant / entrant »).
